# Guides rewrite brief (four guides)

Adapted from the Lux Tuition `seo-content-planner` method. Volumes are average
monthly Australian searches (Keyword Planner, 12 months to Aug 2026).
`competition` is advertiser competition, not organic difficulty.

## Coverage check

Not run. The connected Search Console account holds only Lux Tuition
properties; Bush Gums is not verified. The site is unindexed, so
cannibalisation risk is limited to our own four guides (boundaries below).

## Discrepancies

- "how to choose a hiking tent" and all "first/beginner" phrasings show 0-40
  volume despite being the old titles.
- "pad" dominates R-value searches while "mat" dominates product searches;
  the mat guide uses both.
- No Search Console data (see coverage check).
