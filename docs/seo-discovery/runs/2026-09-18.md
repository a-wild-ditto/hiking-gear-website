## Accuracy flags

- Region guides: never invent trips, campsites visited or conditions (no
  proof points). Cite park authorities (NSW NPWS, Parks Victoria, Parks and
  Wildlife Tasmania) for bookings, closures and fire rules.
- Stove and fire-season content: fire-ban rules on gas and liquid-fuel stoves
  differ by state and change during total fire bans. Verify against the state
  fire authority before publishing any rule.
- Water treatment: no guarantee that a filter makes water safe; attribute
  performance claims to manufacturers.
- Competitors: comparisons allowed, never named; use "traditional retailers".
- Brand terms like "nature hike cloud up 2" are product names we review, not
  competitor brands, so targeting them is allowed.
- Spelling: en-AU throughout; "sleeping pad" only in R-value contexts.
