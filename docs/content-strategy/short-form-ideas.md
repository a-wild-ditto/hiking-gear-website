# Short-form ideas: 55 Shorts, Reels and TikToks

## Why short-form leads, not follows

The single strongest evidence point in the whole research programme is a 283-subscriber
Australian channel (Bec Cliffe) getting **42,000 views on a Short titled `$64 vs $570 Rain
Pants for Hiking`**. That is a 148x view-to-subscriber ratio, on a channel with no audience,
in the Australian hiking niche, using nothing but a numeric price contrast.

Her second-best performer, `GEAR REVIEW: ULTRALIGHT HIKING BACKPACK`, is also a Short at 20K
(71x). Her destination long-form videos sit under 750 views.

**The operational consequence:** Bush Gums should not treat Shorts as offcuts from long-form.
Shorts are the primary discovery mechanism available to a channel with no subscribers, and
the long-form video is the destination. Every field trip should be filmed with the Shorts
list in hand.

## Hook rules derived from the evidence

1. **A number in the first two seconds or it does not work.** `$64 vs $570` beat everything.
2. **Two prices beat one price.** Contrast outperforms magnitude.
3. **Do not make a spec sheet.** The format that failed for Bec Cliffe was the informational
   one. The format that worked was the comparison.
4. **One idea per Short.** If it needs two sentences of setup, it is a long-form segment.
5. **End on the surprise, not on a summary.** Loop back to the opening number.
6. **CTA only where there is a real destination.** Roughly one Short in three. Constant
   calls to action suppress the format.

## Production notes

**Capture ratio.** These 55 Shorts come from 20 long-form videos, but they are not extracted
in edit. Roughly 60 percent of them require a deliberate second take at capture time:
a different framing, a timer running, a scale in shot, or a reaction recorded separately.
Filming the long-form video and hoping Shorts fall out of it produces weak Shorts.

**The scale and the timer are the two most valuable props Bush Gums owns.** Ideas 13, 23,
25, 35, 39, 52, 53 and 54 all depend on a visible number changing on camera. Buy a digital
luggage scale with a clear display and keep it in the pack.

**Vertical from the start.** Shorts 20, 21, 22, 23 and 29 cannot be recovered from
horizontal long-form footage without cropping away the subject. Shoot a vertical pass.

**Publish Shorts ahead of the long-form video, not after.** With no subscriber base, the
Short is the discovery event and the long-form is the destination. Bec Cliffe's data shows
the Short reaching 148x her subscriber count while her long-form sat at 2.5x.

**Do not put a call to action on more than about a third of them.** The Shorts that broke
out in the competitor set were self-contained.
